Online rules

Two saved 15-card decks. Maximum three copies of each meme. Cards keep their fixed facing. Drag a card onto a free cell or select it and confirm. Escape cancels your selection.

Optional: move the blockade once per player per round. Element advantage breaks tied edge values; it adds no point. Event fields and card abilities use the same rules for both players.

90 seconds per turn, measured by the server. At the deadline, the server places a legal card. Selections, animation and blockade moves do not extend the deadline.

Two rounds with the same fields, reshuffled decks and switched initiative. The two final board scores are added. Each side plays nine cards per round. A reconnect restores the shared board.

Only completed Random rounds earn title progress and 5/2 Pixels. Private invitations, AI, deck tests and unfinished rounds do not count toward titles. No bot replaces a Random opponent.
