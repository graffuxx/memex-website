Online rules

First to 2 round wins. Draws do not count. Two saved 15-card decks. Each meme once, with up to two Iconics and one Prime. Cards keep their fixed facing. Drag a card onto a free cell or select it and confirm. Escape cancels your selection.

Optional: move the blockade once per player per round. Element advantage breaks tied edge values; it adds no point. Event fields are currently disabled. Card abilities use the same rules for both players.

90 seconds per turn, measured by the server. At the deadline, the server places a legal card. Selections, animation and blockade moves do not extend the deadline.

First to 2 round wins. Draws do not count. Each new round reshuffles both decks and alternates the starting player. Leaving a matched Random duel counts as a defeat; private exits are unranked. Each side plays nine cards per round. A reconnect restores the shared board.

Completed Random rounds against humans or AI earn 5/2 Pixels, play/win quest progress and 100 XP per win. Skill-based PvP titles and referral qualification require human opponents. Private invitations, AI, deck tests and unfinished rounds do not count toward titles. An empty Random queue uses a labelled AI opponent after 35 seconds. Training, private games, forfeits and unfinished rounds do not advance play/win quests. AI backup does not advance skill titles or referral qualification.
